# SAP S/4HANA Sales and Distribution (SD) – Functional Portfolio

## About This Project

This repository showcases my practical learning and hands-on configuration experience in SAP S/4HANA Sales and Distribution (SD).

It documents key SAP SD business processes, organizational structures, master data, pricing configuration and third-party sales scenarios through configuration explanations, transaction codes, IMG paths and SAP GUI screenshots.

## Project Objectives

- Understand SAP SD organizational structures and assignments.
- Explore Business Partner and Material Master data.
- Understand and execute the Order-to-Cash (OTC) process.
- Configure and understand pricing using the condition technique.
- Understand third-party sales and its integration with procurement.
- Document practical configuration activities and business scenarios.

## Modules Covered

| No. | Module | Key Areas |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Enterprise Structure | Company Code, Sales Organization, Distribution Channel, Division, Plant, Shipping Point and Storage Location |
| 02 | Master Data | Business Partner, Customer Master and Material Master |
| 03 | Order-to-Cash (OTC) | Inquiry, Quotation, Sales Order, Delivery, Picking, PGI and Billing |
| 04 | Pricing | Condition Tables, Access Sequences, Condition Types, Pricing Procedures and Condition Records |
| 05 | Third-Party Sales | Third-Party Sales Order, Purchase Requisition and Document Flow |
